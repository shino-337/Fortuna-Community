### 2. **Features Section**

Features:

Heading: "Built for the Hostile Network"

Features:

   → Enforce strict network policies and mTLS automatically.

**Rationale**: 
- **"Hostile Network"**: More dramatic, security-focused positioning
- **Zero Trust Security**: Industry-standard term, signals modern security practices
- **Live Threat Detection**: AI-driven, proactive stance
- **Multi-Cluster Control**: Emphasizes enterprise scalability

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### Original Vision (from landing-page folder):

The landing page was designed to convey:

1. **High-Stakes Security**: "Hostile Network", "Zero Trust", "Threat Detection"
2. **Enterprise-Grade**: "500+ enterprises", "Multi-Cluster Control"
3. **Proactive Defense**: "See before it becomes an incident", "Catch instantly"
4. **Technical Sophistication**: "Initialize System", "mTLS", "AI-driven"

### Why These Changes Matter:

- **Target Audience**: DevOps/SecOps teams at enterprises with security-critical workloads
- **Positioning**: Not just a management tool, but a security platform
- **Trust Signals**: Social proof (500+ enterprises), industry terms (Zero Trust, mTLS)
- **Differentiation**: Proactive threat detection vs. reactive logging
