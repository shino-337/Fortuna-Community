# Code of Conduct

Fortuna contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces professional, respectful, and focused on technical collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive and specific when giving feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and operational environments.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Handle security-sensitive information privately.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials, infrastructure details, exploit steps, or vulnerability reports in public channels.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask to refocus.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates this code of conduct and may restrict participation when needed to protect the project community.
